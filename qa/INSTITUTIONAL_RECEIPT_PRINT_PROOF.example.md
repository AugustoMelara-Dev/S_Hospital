# Institutional receipt print proof

Copy this file to `qa/INSTITUTIONAL_RECEIPT_PRINT_PROOF.md` on the final cashier computer,
delete this instruction block, and fill every required field below. Do not mark
`PRODUCTION_READY` until media carta, carta and A5 output are validated on the
physical printer or exact printer configuration used by the hospital.

Do not rename required field labels. The production preflight validates these
exact labels.

Keep evidence anonymous. Use role, position or initials in `Responsible person`;
do not write full staff names, patient names, usernames, passwords, `.env`
values or local paths. For `Invoice used`, record only a safe invoice reference
or anonymized test reference, not a patient name.
